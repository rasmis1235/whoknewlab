Who Knew Lab pipeline

A private publishing tool for one YouTube channel

The Who Knew Lab pipeline is an internal tool used by the owner of the Who Knew Lab YouTube channel, which publishes animated, sourced educational videos about science and animal facts.

▶ Watch Who Knew Lab on YouTube

What it does

  • Uploads the channel owner's own finished videos to YouTube as private or scheduled, with title, description, tags and a custom thumbnail.
  • Reads the channel owner's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention) to see where viewers stop watching and improve the next video.

It is not a public app, has no other users, collects no data from anyone else, and is not sold or offered to anyone. It runs on the owner's own computer and signs in only with the owner's own Google account (OAuth 2.0).
